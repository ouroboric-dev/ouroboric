## Challenge

The project will be promoted later through a blog or a series of posts about the journey. Posting
isn't part of v0, but the material has to exist by then.

## Context

Results you didn't record at the time can't be reconstructed later, and reasoning fades fastest.

## Options

Start records when promotion begins, or keep a journal from v0.

## Decision

A journal from v0: one entry per challenge (challenge, context, options, decision, result, lessons),
`ygg metrics --compare` for measured results, and these seed entries written while the design
sessions were still fresh. A future content component (Bragi) will turn entries into posts.

## Result

These entries.

## Lessons

Scrub before anything is public: a ledger line with an API key or a client's private link, pasted
into a post, stays in archives long after the post is edited.
