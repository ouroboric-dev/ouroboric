# Logo concepts

The proposals and explorations behind the [logo](../README.md), kept to show what could have been and
to start the next iteration. **Only the files in the parent folder are the logo.** Everything here is
a concept, and the same trademark rules apply.

## Logo proposals

Three proposals from the founder, traced to vectors and recoloured to the palette.

| Concept | Idea | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `concept-1-carved-knot` | angular scales that interlock like carved Nordic knotwork | **chosen**: the most Nordic and the most distinctive |
| `concept-2-smooth-scales` | a solid ring of rounded scales | the cleanest silhouette, but the least Nordic |
| `concept-3-ring-of-blocks` | the snake through a ring of rounded blocks, like steps on a loop | the closest to the first concept and the factory story; it breaks up at small sizes and can read as a gear |

Each has a light file and a `-dark` file.

## Favicon explorations

In order. Each step answered a problem with the one before it.

| File | What it tried | Why it moved on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `favicon-1-ring` | a plain ring with an orange arc | read as a loading spinner |
| `favicon-2-segments` | the ring cut into scales, a round head | head and tail too round for the carved logo |
| `favicon-3-nodes` | a thin ring with blocks | too busy at 16 px |
| `favicon-4-carved-four-scales` | carved scales, a pointed head, a pointed tail | the head looked like a hawk's at first; flattened |
| `favicon-5-carved-three-scales` | fewer scales, wider gaps | head centred at 12 o'clock; the logo's sits to the right |
| `favicon-6-carved-eye` | an eye in the head | the eye only shows from 32 px up |
| `favicon-7-fitted` | head, eye and tail measured from the logo; the tail fills the open mouth | a strong candidate; its tail lacked the logo's gate |
| `favicon-8-founder-tuned` | the founder's drawing, cropped tight, thin gaps closed, an eye added | kept the gate; its body and head were rougher than the fitted one |
| `favicon-9-founder-drawn` | the founder's drawing as traced | strong from 32 px; at 16 px its thin gaps turned into grey specks |

The final favicon combines the fitted head and body (7) with the founder's gated tail (8).
